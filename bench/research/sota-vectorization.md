Verdict: for photo-like fidelity per primitive the leaders are splatting-style optimisers (Bezier Splatting, then Vector Scaffolding and CubicSplat) and, for step cost, Windfoil. The VLM family is not competitive on arbitrary images. Cross-paper numbers are not comparable (different iterations, sizes, seeds). Windfoil's BS figure (22.80 dB Kodak, 512 shapes, 800 steps) is far below BS's own paper (25.61 closed to 27.90 open), so that comparison reflects a short budget.

## 2. Recommended recipe (single best for browser)

Core: Windfoil renderer + Vector-Scaffolding schedule + Bezier-Splatting/LIVE regularisers. This is a synthesis; no paper combines them.

1. Primitive: closed path of 2-3 cubics (BS/CubicSplat: 10 control points), flat RGB + alpha, fixed z-order. Windfoil needs quadratics: split each cubic into quadratics when uploading to GPU (its benchmark uses 8 quadratic segments per shape). Gradients: per-layer linear gradient fills (as Victor already does).
2. Renderer: Windfoil box-filter winding per pixel ([ALGORITHM.md](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/texel-org/windfoil-algorithm/main/docs/ALGORITHM.md)): split quadratics at x/y extrema into monotone pieces (CPU), bin into row bands, solve the y-window then x-zones by closed-form roots, INSIDE zone area = 2d X Y' + (2d^3/3)(a2x Y' + 2 a2y X'), RIGHT zone = sx*dy, F = W/(sx sy), coverage = min(|F|,1). Painter order source-over, opacity x coverage. Inexact at self-crossings and overlapping contours, so keep the Xing penalty.
3. Gradients: hand-written VJP (Windfoil): coverage gradient equals the outline normal, per monotone piece the chord rotated 90 degrees ([NOTES.md](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/texel-org/windfoil-algorithm/main/docs/NOTES.md)). Recompute forward per tile in backward; accumulate with fixed-point integer atomics (order independent; watch overflow). Cheaper-to-build alternative: BS Gaussians-on-curve splatting for optimisation, verified with exact coverage.
4. Init (Scaffolding, claimed): N0=16 large blobs, spawn new curves where a Gaussian-blurred squared-error map is high, radius halves per generation (R_k=0.5 R_{k-1}), count doubles every 100 iterations (16, 32, ..., 1024 by iteration 600), newer on top. Alternatives: LIVE component init (error >0.1 quantised to 200 bins, centre of mass of largest connected component, circle of 5 px) or SGLIVE (Laplacian + Otsu + watershed on the residual, 4-cubic circle at region centre). In Victor, init from VTracer stacked layers and refine (already the repo's approach).
5. Loss: MSE + 0.01*alpha-to-1 + 0.02*Xing + 1.0*BS-reg (Scaffolding Eq. 6); LIVE Xing 0.01, SGLIVE 0.05; LIVE UDF weighting (tau=10) emphasises contours (optional).
6. Optimiser: Adan (betas .98/.92/.99) per Scaffolding and CubicSplat, or Adam. Scaffolding lr: control points 0.01, colour 0.5, opacity 0.5 (a 50x raise over BS 2e-4 / 0.01 / 0.1; claimed key to speed). CubicSplat: colour/opacity 0.1, points 1e-3, width 1e-4. Pixel-unit parametrisations (LIVE/SGLIVE/O&R) use lr 1 on points, with step caps.
7. Schedule: 5k iterations (Scaffolding) vs 10-15k (BS). Prune opacity <= 0.01 at doubling steps and respawn. BS: every 400 its prune opacity < 0.02, faint middle segment, AABB IoU > 0.9 with colour diff < 0.03, stop for last 1000 its. CubicSplat: every 500 its Gini-of-visibility > 0.35, off for last 2000 (ablation: removing it costs 1.1 to 3.6 dB).
8. Export: one `<path d="M..C..Z" fill="#rrggbb" fill-opacity>` per curve in z-order, nonzero fill, matching the painter-order compositing (BS exports sigmoid colour, opacity 1 for closed paths). Compaction: integer coordinates in a viewBox with ~0.1-0.25 px precision, relative `c`/`s`/`h`/`v`, short hex, drop alpha > 0.98, merge same-colour neighbours, byte-priced pruning (Victor already does the last).

## 3. Failure modes and fixes
- Hairline seams between abutting shapes (AA conflation): overlap or stack layers rather than tile, merge same-colour neighbours ([Inkscape forum](https://www.inkscape.org/forums/questions/two-shapes-next-to-each-other-a-thin-line); no authoritative paper found). Windfoil filters per shape before compositing, which differs from filtering the composite where edges meet (its stated limitation), so validate against the browser rasteriser.
- Self-crossing winding error in Windfoil (bright seam at rosette crossings): Xing loss, avoid even-odd.
- Topology collapse / polygon soup from flat high-lr optimisation (Scaffolding): coarse-to-fine generations with interior-gradient aggregation.
- BS: phase-sensitive aliasing, blurred thick strokes, spikes at junctions (per CubicSplat and NURBS papers); LPIPS can reward texture-like artefacts.
- Stroke vs fill editability: BS open curves need 3 opacities and a width; SVG stroke cannot vary opacity along the stroke, so export strokes as filled outlines or use closed fills only (portable, editable). Convexity/Xing constraint costs ~0.6 dB (CubicSplat ablation).
- Banding on gradients: flat fills need many layers; use per-layer linear/radial gradient fills (SGLIVE: 2-stop radial, linear as degenerate case; BS only mentions linear). SVG has no dithering, so keep per-layer colour step below 1/255.
- Windfoil limits: no strokes, clips, gradients or textures yet; CPU preprocessing; fixed-point overflow.

## 4. Realistic targets (512 px) [my estimates anchored on claimed numbers]
Kodak (768x512): 512 shapes 26.6-27.9 dB (Windfoil 26.60 at 800 steps; Scaffolding 26.68 at 5k; BS open 27.90 at 15k); 1024 shapes 28.3-29.9. DIV2K (harder): 512 shapes 22.6-24.4, 1024 shapes 24.0-25.9. Simple illustration 512x288 at 4096 shapes 26.8-27.9 (Windfoil). Suggested browser goals: 512 shapes ~26 dB on photos in 5-10 s on a mid GPU; 2048 shapes ~29 dB; 4096 ~30. Size (estimate): ~45-60 bytes per compacted closed 2-cubic path, so 512 shapes ~25-30 KB, 2048 ~100-120 KB. Victor's current corpus mean is 474 KB (README, score-based, no PSNR) so byte savings come from path efficiency (CubicSplat: 256 curves match DiffVG at 1024). SuperSVG-F (27.5 dB at 500 paths, 29.1 at 2000, ImageNet) is a sanity bound.

Open items: CubicSplat and SuperSVG licences unstated; Vector Scaffolding has no released code; Windfoil is a single-seed preprint whose authors say the formulation was found by Claude Code; Vector Magic and Vectorizer.AI methods are undisclosed; DiffVG/LIVE primary numbers were taken from the BS and Scaffolding tables, not re-read from their own papers.
